> I recently came across The Pyramid Principle, and as a mom of four young kids, it resonated with me in such a deep, powerful way. The book tells the true story of John Vallely and how he applied John Wooden’s “Pyramid of Success” to his own life, especially during the unimaginable loss of his daughter to cancer. It’s an incredible mix of life lessons, resilience, and adventure that pulls you in from the start. The way John and his wife dealt with their grief felt so raw and relatable. Even though the story centers around their tragic loss, it’s also about strength, growth, and finding purpose in life’s hardest moments. There’s a David vs. Goliath feel to it—a reminder that no matter how overwhelming life’s challenges are, there’s a way to push through. The lessons from the “Pyramid of Success” go beyond sports—they’re lessons for every family and individual trying to navigate life’s ups and downs. For parents especially, it’s a reminder of the importance of character, perseverance, and love. This book is more than a story—it’s a guide to life that left me feeling inspired and ready to face whatever comes next with my own family.

> A story of heartbreak and redemption that will have you riding the roller-coaster of emotions. This story teaches us how God can show us love even in the toughest of times. John Wooden's leadership teachings throughout the book are principles that will no doubt withstand the test of time.

> The book, while initially intimidating due to its sports terminology, turned out to be an easy read and deeply engaging. It's so much more than just an athlete's basketball career journey- it's a powerful love story and an inspirational narrative filled with emotional highs and lows. I found myself celebrating his successes and empathizing with his family's struggles. But of course, the biggest takeaway was the motivating "pyramid of principles," which not only influenced his basketball career but also guided him throughout his life, offering valuable lessons for everyone, including myself. I wish I could've known Coach Wooden personally, but reading this certainly grew my respect for him and all of the wonderful lessons he taught, and even continues to teach through his legacy.

> A riveting and heartfelt journey, The Pyramid Principle captures the incredible resilience of John Vallely and the lasting legacy of Coach John Wooden’s teachings. With profound moments of triumph, love, and loss. Inspiring story moved me with its powerful lessons on the true meaning of success and also made me deeply reflect on my own life, as my mother also passed away from cancer, and it inspires me to support cancer research in her memory. Thank you John Vallely.
